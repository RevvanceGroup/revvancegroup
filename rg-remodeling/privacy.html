Privacy Policy

How R&G Remodeling and Roofing collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how R & G Remodeling, Construction, & Roofing LLC ("R&G," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use rgremodelpgh.com or contact us. By using our website or submitting a form, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the information you choose to give us through our forms, or by calling or texting us, including your name, phone number, city or borough, and details about the project you are asking about. We do not collect payment information through this website.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This is used only to keep the site secure and working properly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your request and schedule a free estimate
  • To contact you about your project by phone, text message, or email
  • To provide the services you ask for and follow up afterward
  • To keep records and improve how we serve customers

Text Messages and Calls

When you provide your phone number, you agree that R&G may call and send text messages to that number about your request and your project. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages, or reply HELP for help. Opting out of texts will not affect your ability to reach us by phone.

We do not share or sell your mobile phone number or your text-messaging consent to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, such as scheduling, customer-management, and communication tools, and only so they can perform work on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights. As stated above, mobile information and text-messaging consent are never shared for third-party marketing.

Cookies

Our website uses only the essential cookies needed for it to function properly. We do not use this website to build advertising profiles or to sell your browsing data.

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to your request, complete your project, and meet our legal and recordkeeping obligations, after which we delete or de-identify it.

Your Choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may also ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by calling or texting (412) 758-4218. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.

Contact Us
