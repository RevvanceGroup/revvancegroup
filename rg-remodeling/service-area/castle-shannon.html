Roofing & Remodeling in Castle Shannon

Castle Shannon is our hometown. We are based right here in the borough, so the roofs, siding, gutters, kitchens and baths we work on are often a few streets away. Local in the most literal sense.

Why Castle Shannon calls us

This Is Our Borough, Not a Service Area on a Map

Castle Shannon is a small borough of about 8,300 people, sitting directly south of Pittsburgh in the South Hills, with the T light rail running through it and a stop right in town. It is also where we are based. When a Castle Shannon homeowner calls, they are not reaching a crew driving in from the far side of the county. They are reaching neighbors who know these streets, this weather, and what these houses are built like.

A lot of Castle Shannon was built in the early to mid 20th century, so the housing stock is older and denser, and plenty of those roofs are at or past the end of their life. We have worked on this kind of home for years. We replace and repair roofs with Owens Corning systems, and most replacements come off and go back on in a single day. The same crew that handles your roof in the warm months builds kitchens and bathrooms through the winter, so you can keep one number for the whole house.
