Roofing & Remodeling in Mt. Lebanon

Mt. Lebanon is one of the established communities in the South Hills, with older homes and steeper, more detailed rooflines, and we are based right next door in Castle Shannon. The kind of older brick Tudors and Colonials Mt. Lebanon is full of need a crew that respects the original roofline, not one rushing through a cookie-cutter tear-off.

Why Mt. Lebanon calls us

We Know What Mt. Lebanon's Older Tudors and Colonials Take

Mt. Lebanon is one of the older, established municipalities in the South Hills, with tree-lined streets, a walkable older core, and the T running through it. A lot of the homes are brick Tudors and Colonials that have been here a long time, which means older roofs and steeper, more detailed rooflines than a newer plan. On that kind of roof the flashing, the valleys and a clean tear-off are where the job is won or lost, and that is the work we do. We replace and repair roofs with Owens Corning systems, and on a steeper Mt. Lebanon pitch we take the extra time the cuts, valleys and flashing details need rather than papering over them.

Those big mature trees that make the streets look the way they do also drop a lot onto a roof, so the gutters fill, the valleys hold debris, and the wear shows up sooner. Being based in Castle Shannon, the next town over, means short drives and a crew that already knows these streets and what these older roofs take. The same people who replace your roof in the summer build kitchens and bathrooms through the winter, so when an older Mt. Lebanon home needs work outside and in, it stays with one crew that already knows the house.
