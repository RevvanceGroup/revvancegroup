Roofing & Remodeling in Bethel Park

Bethel Park is one of the largest communities in the South Hills, and we are based right next door in Castle Shannon. Roofs, siding, gutters, kitchens and baths, handled by a local crew that does not have far to drive.

Why Bethel Park calls us

A South Hills Crew, Not a Number From Across the County

Bethel Park runs from the older streets near the Castle Shannon line out to the newer plans toward South Park, which puts roofs all over the age map: postwar homes on their second or third roof, and newer builds hitting the end of that first builder-grade shingle. We have worked both. We replace and repair roofs with Owens Corning systems, and most replacements come off and go back on in a single day.

Being based in Castle Shannon, the next town over, is not a marketing line. It means short drives, fast free inspections, and a crew that already knows these streets and the weather these roofs take. The same people who replace your roof in the summer build kitchens and bathrooms through the winter, so a Bethel Park homeowner can keep one number for the whole house.
