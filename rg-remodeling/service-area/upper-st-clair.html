Upper St. Clair, PA

Roofing & Remodeling in Upper St. Clair

Upper St. Clair is one of the South Hills' larger-home communities, and we are based nearby in Castle Shannon. The bigger, more cut-up roofs out here take more planning, and that is the work a close-by crew does well.

Why Upper St. Clair calls us

Bigger Roofs Need a Crew That Knows Them

Upper St. Clair is an affluent township of about 20,000 people, and the homes here are bigger, on bigger lots, than much of the South Hills. Bigger homes mean bigger, more complex roofs, with more pitch changes, dormers and valleys to manage. A lot of the housing went up from the mid-20th century onward, and many of those mid-to-late-century roofs are now on their first or second full replacement. That is the work we do, and we plan the job and the cleanup around a larger roof rather than rushing it.

It is not only the older homes. On the newer developments, builder-grade shingles tend to reach the end of their life sooner than owners expect, so plenty of those roofs are due as well. Being based in Castle Shannon, a short drive away, means fast free inspections and a crew that knows the weather these roofs take. We replace and repair these bigger roofs with Owens Corning systems, working the dormers and valleys carefully and matching shingles to a larger roof line instead of treating it like a small ranch. The same people who do your roof in the summer build kitchens and bathrooms through the winter, which is handy on an Upper St. Clair house where there is usually more than one project on the list.
