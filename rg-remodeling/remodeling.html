Questions

Remodeling, Answered

Is it the same crew that does the roofing?

Yes. One owner, one crew. The people who tear off roofs in the summer are the ones building the kitchens and baths in the winter, so you are dealing with the same accountable team either way.

How long does a kitchen or bathroom remodel take?

It depends on the scope. A straightforward bathroom moves faster than a full kitchen with a new layout. We give you a realistic schedule in writing before we start, and we tell you up front about the steps that drive the timeline so there are no surprises.

Do you handle the whole project, or just part of it?

We handle the build from start to finish. Where a specialty trade is involved, we tell you honestly and coordinate it ourselves, so you are not the one chasing down separate contractors.
