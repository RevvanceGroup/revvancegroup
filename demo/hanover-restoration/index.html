Good to know

Remodeling & building questions, answered

Do you work on historic homes?
Yes. In 30 years we have worked on homes of every age and style, including ones dating back to 1775. We restore and update historic homes with respect for their character.
Do you build custom homes from scratch?
We do. As a design-build firm we can take a custom home from first concept through finished construction, along with additions and whole-home renovations.
What is design-build?
Design-build means one team handles your project from design through construction. It keeps you on one timeline with one point of accountability, instead of juggling a separate designer and contractor.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Hanover Restoration & Remodeling is a licensed Class A contractor in Virginia, fully insured, and certified in wedi and Schluter waterproofing systems for tubs and showers.
Do you do aging-in-place remodels?
Yes. We design and build aging-in-place upgrades and accessibility modifications so your home stays safe and comfortable for the long term.
What areas do you serve?
We serve Hanover County and the greater Richmond area, including Mechanicsville, Hanover Courthouse, and Ashland.
