Good to know

Flat roofing questions, answered straight

What flat roof systems do you install?
TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, and built-up systems, plus roof coatings and restoration. We match the system to your building, your roof traffic, and your budget, using GAF, GenFlex, and Mule-Hide products.
Can my flat roof be repaired, or do I need a full replacement?
Often a targeted repair or a roof coating buys you years before a full replacement is needed. After a free inspection we give you an honest assessment of repair versus replace, not a sales pitch.
Do you work on commercial and residential flat roofs?
Yes. We handle commercial, industrial, and residential flat roofs across Hampton Roads, from low-slope home additions to large commercial buildings.
How long does a flat roof last?
It depends on the system and how it is maintained. Many flat roof systems last 15 to 30 years, and a roof coating can extend the life of an existing roof. A free inspection tells you exactly where yours stands.
Are you licensed, insured, and certified?
Yes. Flat Roofs by Pegram is a BBB-accredited business and a manufacturer-certified installer for GAF, GenFlex, and Mule-Hide, with over 50 years serving Hampton Roads.
How fast can you come out for a roof leak?
Flat roof leaks are worth catching early before water spreads under the membrane. Call us at (757) 537-1945 and we will schedule an inspection as soon as possible.
