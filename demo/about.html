About Roofing Handyman Services

Our Story

How Roofing Handyman Services got started

We started Roofing Handyman Services in 2014 because we were tired of watching homeowners in Modesto get burned. Big out-of-state outfits would roll in after a storm, sign people up fast, install a roof in a day, and disappear with the warranty paperwork. When something failed two winters later, nobody picked up the phone.

We figured the better business was the obvious one. Show up when you say you will. Quote what the job actually costs. Do the work right. Put the warranty in writing. Stick around to honor it.

12-plus years later, that is still the whole plan. We are not the cheapest roofer in Modesto. We are not the biggest. We are the one your neighbor tells you about because we did them right.

What We Believe

How we run a roofing job

Tell The Truth

If your roof has five good years left, we tell you. If a repair will buy you time, we offer the repair. We do not sell a replacement to anyone who does not need one.

Quote It Honestly

Line-item pricing on a real estimate. No bait-and-switch. The number we give you is the number you pay, unless we find unexpected damage and we get your approval first.

Put It In Writing

Estimate, contract, workmanship warranty, manufacturer warranty. Everything you need is on paper before we lift a hammer.

Treat The Crew Right

Our installers are employees, not anonymous day-labor. They are trained, background-checked, and they get paid fairly. That is the only way we get consistent quality.

Respect The House

We tarp your landscaping. We protect your AC unit. We do not stomp through your flower beds. We sweep for nails with a magnet before we leave. Your yard looks better than when we got there.

Stick Around

We are based in Modesto. If a shingle blows off two years from now, you can find us. Local phone, local office, local crew. That is the whole point.
