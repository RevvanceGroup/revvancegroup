Good to know

Roofing & siding questions, answered straight

What shingle brands do you install?
We are a certified installer for GAF, Owens Corning, and Atlas, including the Atlas Pinnacle Sun line. You can see and feel every color in our Virginia Beach showroom before you decide.
Do you really have a showroom?
Yes. Our showroom at 829 Lynnhaven Pkwy in Virginia Beach has full shingle and siding displays with pricing right on the wall, so you can compare options in person with no guesswork and no pressure.
Do you do siding and gutters too, or just roofing?
Both. We install roofing, vinyl and fiber cement siding, and seamless gutter systems, so one Class A contractor can handle your whole exterior.
How much does a new roof cost?
It depends on the size and pitch of your roof and the shingle you choose. We give you an itemized written estimate for free, with our pricing published openly so there are no surprises.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Better View is a licensed Class A contractor in Virginia, fully insured, and a manufacturer-certified installer. We are happy to provide proof on request.
What areas do you serve?
We serve all of Hampton Roads, including Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, Norfolk, Portsmouth, and Suffolk.
