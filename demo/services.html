Roofing services for Chico homes and businesses

Replacement, repair, storm response, gutters, and free inspections. Everything we do is backed by a written workmanship warranty.

Roof Replacement

Complete tear-off down to the deck, replace damaged sheathing where needed, install new underlayment, drip edge, valleys, vents, and shingles. Architectural asphalt, metal, and tile available. Manufacturer warranty plus our written workmanship warranty.

When you need it: Roof is at the end of its life, multiple leaks, hail or wind damaged a large portion, or selling your home and the inspector flagged it.

Roof Repair

Leaks, missing or torn shingles, damaged flashing, cracked vent boots, sealing around chimneys and skylights. We diagnose the actual cause, not just patch the visible spot.

When you need it: Active leak, water stain on the ceiling, missing shingles after a storm, or you want to extend the life of a roof that has a few years left.

Storm Damage and Insurance Claims

After hail, wind, or fallen-tree damage, we inspect the roof, photograph and document everything, prepare a damage summary for your insurance company, meet your adjuster on the roof, and handle the back-and-forth so you do not have to. You pay your deductible and that is it.

When you need it: Recent hail or wind storm, visible damage from the ground, or your neighbors are filing claims.

Commercial Roofing

TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, standing-seam metal, and built-up systems. Warehouses, retail strips, restaurants, multifamily, and small offices. Scheduled around your operating hours so we are not in your customers' way.

When you need it: Active commercial leak, end-of-life flat roof, planning a property purchase, or your insurance is asking for a roof certification.

Gutters and Drainage

Seamless aluminum gutters in standard colors. Downspouts sized for your roof. Gutter guards if you want them. Properly pitched so water flows away from your house, not into your foundation.

When you need it: Gutters are sagging, overflowing, pulling away from the fascia, or you are getting basement seepage after rain.

Free Roof Inspection

A technician climbs the roof, takes photos of every slope, identifies any damage, and emails you a plain-English report with recommendations. No upsell, no high-pressure follow-up. If your roof is fine, we tell you it is fine.

When you need it: Buying or selling a home, after a storm rolls through, or you just want to know what shape your roof is in before something goes wrong.

Materials

Roofing materials we install

We help you pick what makes sense for your house, your budget, and the California weather. Not what gives us the biggest markup.

Asphalt Shingles

The standard for most Chico homes. Architectural shingles last 25 to 30 years, hold up in wind, and come in dozens of colors. Best value for most homeowners.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam or stone-coated steel. Lasts 50 plus years. Holds up to hail. Energy efficient in hot California summers. Higher up-front cost, much lower lifetime cost.

Tile Roofing

Clay and concrete tile for the right architectural style. Heavy, requires a properly built structure, but lasts the life of the house and looks the part.

Flat and Low-Slope

TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen for commercial buildings and modern residential designs. Sealed, walkable, and engineered for ponding-water resistance.
