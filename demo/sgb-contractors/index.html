Good to know

Building & remodeling questions, answered

What is the difference between a custom and a modular home?
A custom stick-built home is built entirely on site to your plans. A modular home is built about 80 percent complete in a controlled factory, then delivered and finished on site, which can save time. We build both and help you choose the right fit.
Do you do remodels and renovations, or only new homes?
Both. Alongside custom and modular homes, we handle kitchen and bath remodels, whole-home renovations, restoration, and small commercial projects.
How long has SGB Contractors been in business?
We have been building across Hampton Roads since 2001, and began neighborhood development work in 2006.
What areas do you serve?
We build and remodel across Hampton Roads, including Virginia Beach and Chesapeake and the surrounding communities.
