Good to know

Roofing questions, answered straight

How much does a new roof cost in Stockton, CA?
Most asphalt shingle replacements on an average single family home run between $9,000 and $18,000. Final price depends on roof size, pitch, layers to remove, and material. We give you an itemized written estimate for free, with no obligation.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most residential roofs are torn off and replaced in one to three days, weather permitting. We confirm a clear timeline before we start and protect your landscaping and driveway throughout.
Do you help with storm damage and insurance claims?
Yes. We provide a free storm inspection, document the damage with photos, and meet your insurance adjuster on site. Many homeowners pay only their deductible on an approved claim.
What roofing materials do you install?
Architectural asphalt shingles, standing seam and metal roofing, concrete and clay tile, and flat or low slope systems such as TPO for commercial buildings. We help you pick the right fit for your home and budget.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Ironcrest Roofing Co. is licensed by the California State License Board, fully bonded, and carries general liability and workers compensation insurance. Proof is available on request.
How long should a roof last in the Central Valley?
In Stockton's hot, dry summers, a quality asphalt shingle roof lasts about 20 to 30 years, metal 40 to 70 years, and tile 50 years or more with maintenance. A free inspection tells you exactly where yours stands.
