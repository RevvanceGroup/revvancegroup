Windows & exterior doors

Windows & Doors, Set Right the First Time

A window is only as good as its install. We set them plumb, flash them into the wall system and seal them so Idaho weather stays where it belongs.

Windows

Replacement & New Construction Windows

Drafty single panes, fogged-up double panes with broken seals, or builder-grade units that never slid right: we pull them, fix the opening if it needs it, and set new windows that actually seal.

Because we are a siding crew first, we do not caulk-and-pray over bad flashing. When the siding comes off, your windows get integrated into the house wrap the way the manufacturer drew it up. That is the difference between a window that performs for 20 years and one that leaks at the corners by year three.

  • Vinyl and fiberglass replacement windows
  • New construction windows for builds and additions
  • Egress windows for basement bedrooms
  • Trim, capping and interior finish included in the quote

Exterior doors

Entry, Patio & French Doors

If you have to lift, shove or shoulder-check your door in January, it was not hung right.

We install entry doors, back doors, patio sliders and French doors, set square in the opening with the threshold sealed and the weatherstripping actually touching the door. Hardware installed, gaps even, latch catching on the first push.

The smart play

Bundle Windows and Doors With Siding

When siding and openings are done together, the flashing, wrap and trim all work as one system, and you only pay for one mobilization. If your siding is tired and your windows are too, have us look at both before you commit to either.

One Inspection

We measure the openings and check what is behind the siding at the same visit, then quote the work together and separately so you can compare.

One Crew

No window company blaming the siding company for a leak. The same hands set the window, flash it and close the wall around it.

One Warranty Conversation

Something not right afterward? You call Derek. That is the whole escalation path.
