Soffit & Fascia That Protect Your Roofline

The most ignored boards on the house, and the first place rot, leaks and pests get in. We repair, replace and vent them properly.

The basics

What Soffit and Fascia Actually Do

Fascia is the vertical board that runs along your roof edge, behind the gutters. Soffit is the underside that bridges the gap between the roof edge and the wall. Together they close off your attic from weather, squirrels, wasps and birds, and the soffit's vents feed the airflow that keeps your attic dry in winter and cooler in summer.

When they fail, you usually find out the expensive way: sagging gutters, water stains at the top of a wall, ice damming, or something scratching around in the attic. Catching it early is cheap. Ignoring it is not.

Signs yours need attention

  • Peeling paint or soft, spongy wood along the roof edge
  • Gutters pulling away from the house
  • Stains or streaks under the eaves after a storm
  • Bees, wasps or birds moving in at the roofline
  • An attic that bakes in summer or drips with condensation in winter

How we fix it

Repair Where It Makes Sense, Replace Where It Does Not

We open it up, find how far the damage runs, and give you both numbers when both are real options.

Inspect

We check the full run, not just the spot you can see from the driveway, plus the gutter attachment and attic ventilation path.

Fix the Cause

Rot usually starts with a flashing or gutter problem. We correct what let the water in before we close anything up.

Finish Clean

New aluminum or fiber cement fascia, vented soffit panels matched to the house, sealed and painted so it disappears into the lines of the roof.

Good to know

Often the Smartest Add-On to a Siding Job

If we are already on the wall with scaffolding and trim brake on site, adding soffit and fascia costs far less than a separate visit later. Ask for the combined number in your estimate. Most homeowners are surprised how small the difference is.
