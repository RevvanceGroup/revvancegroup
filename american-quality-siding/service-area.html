Mountain communities

Cabins, Lake Houses & Mountain Builds

Good exterior crews are hard to book once you leave the valley. We make the drive.

Mountain exteriors are a different animal: snow load against the walls, freeze-thaw cycles that destroy bad caulk joints, and intense UV at elevation. We spec materials for that reality, steel siding, LP SmartSide and fiber cement over bare-minimum vinyl, and detail the flashing for snow country.
