Siding Installation, Replacement & Repair

From one cracked panel to a full tear-off and re-side, and every new build in between. Hung straight, flashed right, finished clean.

What you get

Siding Is the Whole Job, Not Just the Panels

Anyone can nail siding to a wall. The difference between a job that looks good for two years and one that protects your house for decades is everything you cannot see when it is done: house wrap, flashing, drip edge, straight starter courses and clean transitions around windows and doors.

That detail work is what Derek's crew is known for. A customer review on Google calls out a 510 foot wainscoting project "using corrugated materials, flashing and drip ledge" and describes the crew as "hard working and precise." That is the standard on every wall we touch.

  • Tear-off, inspection and repair of what is underneath
  • House wrap, flashing and drip edge as one water system
  • Trim, corners and accents that match the style of the house

Materials

Siding We Install

Vinyl Siding

The budget workhorse. Modern vinyl holds color, shrugs off hail and never needs paint. Lap, vertical and shake profiles.

Fiber Cement

Crisp wood look with serious durability. Takes paint beautifully and stands up to sun and sprinkler overspray alike.

LP SmartSide

Engineered wood with real strength against impacts and cold. A favorite for mountain homes and busy family houses.

Steel Siding

The heavyweight for snow country and sun. Color-fast, fire-resistant and tough enough for cabins that sit empty all winter.

Board & Batten

The modern farmhouse look. Vertical lines, deep shadows, great as a full wrap or a gable and entry accent.

Wainscot & Accents

Corrugated metal wainscoting, stone transitions and mixed profiles that give a plain wall some character.

Siding repair

Repairs Welcome. Seriously.

A lot of siding companies will not pick up the phone for anything under a full re-side. We built our reputation on the small stuff.

Wind-peeled panels, woodpecker holes, hail damage, a fascia board going soft, siding bleached chalky on the south wall. We match the existing material as closely as the market allows, fix what caused the failure, and tell you honestly when a repair will not hold and a bigger fix makes more sense.
