Questions

Straight Answers

What areas do you serve around Boise?

We work across the whole Treasure Valley: Boise, Meridian, Eagle, Star, Kuna, Nampa, Caldwell, Middleton, Garden City and Emmett. We also take on projects in the mountain communities, including McCall, Donnelly, Cascade, Tamarack, Garden Valley, Crouch, Horseshoe Bend and Idaho City. Not sure about your area? Call (208) 595-9565 and ask.

What does new siding cost in the Boise area?

As a rough industry range, installed vinyl siding typically runs about $4 to $8 per square foot, engineered wood and fiber cement more like $8 to $14. The honest answer for your house depends on its size, the condition of what is under the old siding, and the material you pick. We look at the job and give you a written number for free, with no pressure to sign anything.

What siding materials do you install?

Vinyl lap and vertical siding, fiber cement, LP SmartSide engineered wood, steel siding, and board and batten. We also do wainscoting accents with corrugated metal, plus the flashing and drip edge details that keep water out. If you have a material in mind, we will tell you straight whether it makes sense for your house and budget.

Do you handle small repairs or only full projects?

Both. Big or small, we do it all. That covers a few wind-damaged pieces of siding, one rotten fascia board, a single door swap, all the way up to wrapping a brand new build. Small repairs done right are how most of our long-term customers found us.

How long does a typical siding job take?

Most repairs are done in a day. A full re-side on an average single family home usually takes about a week once materials are on site, depending on the size of the house, the material and the weather. We give you a clear timeline with your written estimate and we show up when we say we will.

Can you replace windows and doors at the same time as siding?

Yes, and it is usually the smart way to do it. Replacing windows and doors while the siding is open lets us flash and seal everything as one system, which is better for keeping weather out, and it saves you paying for two separate trips. One crew handles siding, windows, doors, soffit and fascia.
